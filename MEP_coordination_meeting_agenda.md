# MEP Coordination Meeting Agenda

Document: MEP_coordination_meeting_agenda.md
Related: MEP_requirements_checklist.md, MEP_equipment_schedule.csv

Purpose
-------
Align project stakeholders (architect, MEP engineers, facilities, and instructional leads) on MEP scope, constraints, routing strategy, and deliverables required to begin schematic MEP design for the 24‑student Automotive & Diesel Mechanics Training Facility.

Meeting Logistics
-----------------
- Suggested duration: 90 minutes
- Format: In‑person preferred; hybrid video call available
- Location: Project conference room (or virtual link)
- Organizer / Lead: Project Manager / Facilities

Required Attendees
------------------
- Architect (lead)
- Mechanical Engineer (HVAC & extraction specialist)
- Electrical Engineer (power distribution & EV charging)
- Plumbing / Fire Protection Engineer
- Facilities Manager / Maintenance Lead
- Instructional Lead (program use, equipment owner)
- Structural Engineer (for lifts/heavy equipment questions)
- Project Manager
- Document Control / Scribe

Pre‑Reads (distribute at least 72 hours prior)
--------------------------------------------
1. `MEP_requirements_checklist.md`
2. `MEP_equipment_schedule.csv`
3. Architectural floor plan (latest issued set)
4. Master DXF reference (training_facility_plan_layered.dxf) and PDF previews

Agenda (90 minutes)
--------------------
0. Intro & Objectives (5 min)
   - Meeting goals, decision outcomes expected, review timeline for schematic design

1. Review Project Assumptions (10 min)
   - Occupancy, bay counts, shift patterns, operational constraints
   - Confirm fixed equipment / bay IDs from the equipment schedule

2. Electrical Overview & Constraints (20 min)
   - Provisional loads per bay; discuss 120/240/480V needs
   - EV charging strategy and spare capacity
   - Overhead vs floor drops; preferred routing
   - Panel and subpanel placement (mechanical/electrical rooms)
   - Decisions required: main service sizing approach; reserve capacity target (confirm 20–30%)

3. HVAC & Extraction (20 min)
   - Diesel exhaust capture approach (rails vs arms), CFM targets, make‑up air
   - Source capture for welding / localized contaminants
   - Zoning strategy (shop vs electronics lab vs admin)
   - Battery storage ventilation and monitoring requirements
   - Decisions required: extraction system topology, make‑up air strategy, CO/NOx sensor placement

4. Plumbing, Waste & Compressed Air (10 min)
   - Trench drains and wash bay routing; oil/water separator strategy
   - Compressed air central vs local compressors; condensate management
   - Decisions required: drain routing, waste oil tank location

5. Fire, Life Safety & Environmental (10 min)
   - Suppression approach in bays, CO monitoring, battery fire response planning
   - Emergency shutoffs and isolation points
   - Decisions required: detection strategy and integration with BMS/alarms

6. Routing & Coordination Constraints (10 min)
   - Overhead corridors, slab penetrations, trench coordination
   - Structural concerns for lifts and heavy equipment
   - Decisions required: overhead corridor alignment and slab penetration zones

7. Actions, Owners, and Schedule (5 min)
   - List action items, deliverables, and owners; confirm next design milestone

Decisions Required (summary)
----------------------------
- Confirm main service sizing policy and spare capacity percentage
- Confirm extraction topology (rail vs arms) for diesel bays
- Confirm EV charger sizing approach and allowance for future expansion
- Confirm preferred routing approach (overhead vs in‑floor) for power/air/fluids
- Confirm locations for major penetrations, waste oil tank, and mechanical/electrical rooms
